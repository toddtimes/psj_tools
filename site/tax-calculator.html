Profit and Tax Totals

Add a Profit Report and a Sales Tax Report. This adds up gross profit for your retail and services categories and reads total taxable sales.

Monthly base costs

Fixed monthly costs the Profit Report doesn't include, such as the rent behind Mailbox Rentals. Each one is multiplied by the months the report covers and taken off that department's profit. Saved in this browser.
