Post San Juan Tools

Back-office helpers for the store. Each one reads its report PDFs right in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

  • CMRA Mailbox Counts

    Drop in a CMRA Quarterly Report to count boxes rented at period end, newly rented, and closed during the quarter.

  • Mailbox Name Labels

    Turn a Mailnames Report into a printable PDF of box name labels, 8 per Letter sheet.

  • Profit and Tax Totals

    Add a Profit Report and a Sales Tax Report to total retail and services gross profit and taxable sales.

Works in current browsers: Chrome or Edge 140 and later, Firefox 134 and later, and Safari 18.2 and later (iPhone and iPad on iOS 18.2 or later). Older browsers can open the pages but cannot read the report PDFs.

These tools are built for reports exported from PostalMate. They are provided free and as-is, with no warranty of any kind, and Post San Juan accepts no liability for their results. Always check the figures against your original reports before relying on them for USPS, tax or other filings.
